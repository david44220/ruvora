# Product

Ruvora is an independent monetization and gamification layer for the social web. Its core promise is “Turn your social presence into an economy.” It helps smaller creators establish a public home, bring their audiences, participate in legitimate advertising activity and join shared events.

“Monetize your audience. From follower #10.” describes an initial configurable eligibility threshold. It must never imply guaranteed income. Real eligibility and allocations depend on activity, advertiser budgets, validation, trust, geography and sustainable economics.

## People and roles

| Role       | Product purpose                                                                       |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| User       | Discover opportunities, join events, follow progression and understand their activity |
| Creator    | Publish a Ruvora Link, bring an audience and access eligible creator participation    |
| Advertiser | Create reviewed campaigns and fund genuine validated engagement                       |
| Admin      | Review campaigns/activity, manage risk, inspect accounting and govern distributions   |

Accounts can hold several roles. User interfaces help people navigate their work; server permissions determine what they may do.

## Ruvora Link and loops

The public `/@handle` profile is the main viral surface: identity, bio, social links, custom links, audience disclosure and discoverable events. It should be desirable to share and exceptional on mobile. Creator content is user-authored; audience information is self-declared until a trusted verification path exists.

The creator loop brings a creator's audience to a Ruvora Link, some of whom participate and later create their own links. Events encourage legitimate shared participation and sharing. Advertisers sponsor meaningful opportunities. Referral attribution is direct and bounded; account creation itself does not generate a reward.

## Four distinct concepts

| Concept           | Meaning                                                      | Never described as                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Money             | Exact, ledger-backed monetary accounting                     | A mutable UI balance or guaranteed earning     |
| Reward Units (RU) | Eligible validated contribution within a distribution period | Money, cryptocurrency or a fixed exchange rate |
| XP                | Long-term progression                                        | Revenue-sharing weight or cash                 |
| Event Points      | Ranking within one event                                     | Cash, XP or universal RU                       |

Advertiser RU comes from validated delivery. Funding/deposits produce zero final RU. The Global Distribution Pool uses configurable participant categories and versioned rules. The Margin Governor protects liabilities, reserves and retained operating margin.

## First implementation boundary

The first vertical slice centers on identity/onboarding, public profiles, role dashboards, campaign lifecycle, pending activity and independent validation, separate RU/XP/Event Points, events/rankings, ledger, distribution preview/finalization and operational documentation. [RELEASE_REPORT.md](RELEASE_REPORT.md) is authoritative for what is completed and verified.

All seeded financial data and campaign credits are development simulation. Live payments, withdrawals, trusted external conversion measurement, social verification and transactional email delivery are unavailable. Unsupported capabilities should be absent or clearly disabled instead of represented by dead controls.

## Design

The official direction is luxury warm futurism: obsidian, warm ivory, champagne, amber and copper. The bespoke interlocking glass-and-metal energy orb family is canonical. Reuse the selected raster originals through responsive delivery variants. Art creates the visual world, the interface composes it, and restrained code-driven motion enhances it.

The built-in image generation tool supplied the actual assets; its interface did not expose a model selector. The repository must not claim a specific image model version it cannot verify. See the visual asset manifest.

## Success and guardrails

Track legitimate activation, profile sharing, creator retention, validated advertiser outcomes, event participation, invalid-traffic rates, support burden and retained operating profit. Financial targets belong in configurable internal reporting, never public earning promises or hard-coded payout rules.

The product must remain an independent creator platform. Avoid paid-click framing, gambling, speculative currency language, uncontrolled referral compensation, fake analytics and artificial social verification.
